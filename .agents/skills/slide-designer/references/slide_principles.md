# Principles of Executive Presentation & Slide Design

This document details the cognitive psychology, corporate consulting standards, and venture capital frameworks behind world-class presentation design.

---

## 1. Cognitive Load Theory & The 3-Second Rule

Audience attention is scarce. When a slide appears on screen, human eyes scan in an **F-pattern** or **Z-pattern**:
1. **The 3-Second Test:** The audience must understand the core takeaway of the slide within 3 seconds.
2. **Dual-Coding Theory (Paivio):** Humans process verbal information and visual information through separate cognitive channels. If a presenter is speaking while the slide contains dense text paragraphs, the channels collide and cause cognitive overload ("Document Syndrome").
3. **Action Headlines vs Category Labels:**
   - *Bad:* "Marketing Mix (4Ps)" (Forces the audience to read everything to understand the point).
   - *Good:* "กลยุทธ์ 4Ps: ฟรีค่าระบบช่วงแรก เก็บ GP ต่ำเพียง 3-5% เพื่อจูงใจร้านค้า" (The conclusion is delivered upfront).

## 2. Structural Frameworks

### A. The Pyramid Principle (Barbara Minto / McKinsey)
- **Top-Down Communication:** Start with the answer/recommendation first.
- Group supporting arguments logically (MECE: Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive).
- Provide quantitative proof points at the bottom of the pyramid.

### B. Sequoia Capital Pitch Deck Flow (15-Slide Master Blueprint)
1. **Company Purpose:** Define the company in one single, declarative sentence.
2. **Problem:** State the customer pain point in its authentic, grounded context.
3. **Solution:** Show how your product solves this problem elegantly.
4. **Why Now?:** The technological, social, or economic inflection point.
5. **Market Size (TAM/SAM/SOM):** Profile the realistic addressable customer base.
6. **Product / Demo:** Step-by-step user journey showing simplicity.
7. **Business Model (BMC):** How you acquire users, monetize, and manage costs.
8. **Competition & Differentiation:** Positioning matrix showing why alternatives fail.
9. **Go-to-Market Strategy (GTM):** Customer acquisition channels and viral loops.
10. **Team:** Why this team is uniquely positioned to execute the vision.
11. **Financials & Milestones:** Revenue projections, break-even timeline, and roadmap.

## 3. Visual Execution Best Practices

### A. The 60-30-10 Color Rule
- **60% Neutral Background:** Light canvas `#FFFFFF` or `#F8FAFC`. Avoid dark backgrounds for printouts and handouts.
- **30% Structural Frame:** Dark slate text `#0F172A`, slate borders `#E2E8F0`, muted subtitles `#64748B`.
- **10% Focal Accent:** Use vivid colors (`#10B981` Emerald, `#2563EB` Royal Blue, `#EA580C` KMUTNB Orange) strictly for key stats, badges, and primary highlights.

### B. Typography & Micro-Layouts
- **Font Pairing:** Use modern geometric or humanist sans-serifs (`Leelawadee UI`, `Segoe UI`, `Inter`).
- **Typographic Scale:**
  - Hero Numbers: `32pt - 44pt` bold
  - Slide Titles: `22pt - 26pt` bold
  - Card Titles: `13pt - 15pt` bold
  - Body Text: `11pt - 12pt` regular
  - Metadata: `9pt - 10pt` muted
- **Lead with Bold:** Always bold the first 2-3 words of any bullet point (e.g. "**ลดเวลารอ:** สั่งล่วงหน้าผ่านไลน์").

### C. Layout Diversity
- Avoid repetitive vertical boxes across slides.
- Use horizontal process chevrons for workflows.
- Use 2x2 Bento Grids for multi-dimensional frameworks (e.g. 4Ps, SWOT).
- Use big metric callout cards for traction and financials.
